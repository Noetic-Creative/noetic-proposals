A market that already knows WSC Sports.

03 · Scope

What Noetic runs.

Media

Paid social, Meta + LinkedIn

Company level targeting build and delivery optimization across both platforms, monitored on an ongoing basis. Review cadence is weekly at launch, moving to biweekly, excluding low value segments as delivery signals come in.

Media

YouTube

Storytelling and retargeting layer built on behavioral proxies: video production affinity, technology industry signals, and OTT in-market interest.

Organic

Organic boosting

Up to 3 organic posts per quarter boosted for extended reach beyond the always on paid layer, timed to the monthly hero moment.

Operations

Account management

Run by the same pod already managing the lead generation retainer, so strategy for both workstreams stays coordinated rather than siloed.

Content

Copywriting

Up to 10 copy pieces per month, covering new copy plus edits and tweaks to existing assets, tied to that month's hero message.

Creative

Design, statics only

Up to 8 statics per month, in 1:1, 4:5 and 9:16, with up to 2 rounds of revisions each. Adaptations or new creations supporting that month's message. Video is supplied by WSC.

Measurement

Reporting & dashboards

Campaign added to the existing account dashboards, plus an end of month report covering the key metrics for this workstream.
