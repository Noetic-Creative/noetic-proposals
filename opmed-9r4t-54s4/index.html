Opmed
After Nurture

What happens when a nurture flow ends and nobody has booked a demo.

The goal

Every contact who leaves Nurture A or B lands somewhere on purpose.

Right now the flows just stop. Nobody is routed, nobody is measured. This fixes both at once: a rule that sends every exiting contact down one of two paths, and a way to see, quarter over quarter, whether that rule is working.

The decision

Two paths. What they clicked decides which one.

Engaged

Goes to a person

Two clicks across the flow, or a second form submission. A real rep follows up, not another email.

Quiet

Goes to a reset

No clicks means the message is not landing. A short, three-email flow asks a different question instead of repeating the same one.

Opens are not counted here. Only clicks and form submissions, until deliverability supports more.

The rule

Four stops, ordered by how warm the contact still is.

Sales Ready

Clicked twice across the flow, or submitted a second form.

Hand to rep

Warm

Exactly one click. Interested, but not asking yet.

Rep if there's room

Passive

Opened something in the flow, never clicked.

Re-engagement

Dormant

No engagement recorded anywhere in the sequence.

Suppress, quarterly

Sales Ready and Warm messages carry a rep's name and a real reply-to, so they read like a person is following up, because one is about to. The actual handoff still runs on lead score, the same way it does today. This is not a new process, just a clearer front door to it.

A hard bounce or unsubscribe ends the sequence immediately, at any stop.

Re-engagement, for Passive contacts

Three short emails. One question, asked once.

This is not more nurture, it is a timing reset. It starts 30 days after the exit, so the contact gets a real gap first, and it closes with a clean answer either way.

Handoff, and how we will measure it

A click creates a task, and a rep has a clock on it.

When a Sales Ready contact clicks, HubSpot notifies the account and opens a task in Salesforce, so the moment is not lost. A rep responds inside 24 business hours; if nothing has moved after three days, the contact drops into Warm treatment automatically.

Turn on Salesforce Activity Synca toggle in the existing integration, no new cost
Get hard bounce under 1%currently 2.6%, before any send goes out
Confirm tracking on the form pagesso a page visit becomes a usable signal

Two things we watch from here: the share of Sales Ready contacts a rep reaches inside that 24-hour window, and the rate at which they turn into a booked meeting, tracked for visibility, not held as a Noetic number, since what happens after handoff depends on the rep, not the email.
