Section 3 · Strategy

Audience strategy & delivery optimization

Target broadly at company level, then optimize from real delivery signals.

01 · Target

Company-level reach

Broadcasters, leagues, federations, clubs, OTT and sports media targeted at the Company level or Industry level.

No persona split, over-restricting targeting would hurt delivery efficiency by increasing CPMs.

02 · Retarget

Reinforce Positioning

Retargeting layer to ensure not only brand awareness but also effective consideration.

03 · Optimize

React to delivery

Monitor (weekly at first, bi-weekly basis later on) which companies, functions or other segments are absorbing spend.

Exclude anything clearly low-value rather than restricting upfront.

(Proven tactic - already doing this on lead gen campaigns)

Section 4 · Channel Rationale

Channel Rationale: where and why

Each channel plays a distinct role in the same awareness system.

Primary

LinkedIn

Primary channel due to it's B2B targeting capabilities.

Also the channel where the account has the most targeting maturity already.

Efficiency

Meta

Proven this year on the lead-gen side, meaningfully lower CPMs, video-first format matches the short-form assets well, and reaches people who don't check LinkedIn as often but scroll Instagram daily regardless of seniority.

Storytelling

YouTube

Best channel for tech-product awareness storytelling (long-form testimonial format works there, results proved it).

Scoped in only as budget allows (Plan B) or retargeting-only (Plan A).

03 · YouTube

Audience built on behavioral proxies: Video Editing & Production Software usage, Technology industry affinity, large/very large employer signals, and an Over-the-Top Media in-market interest to catch the streaming side.

Section 7 · Strategy — Funnel & Creative

Funnel: From cold reach to case study click

A simple sequence: reach broadly, then deepen consideration.

01 · Cold Reach

Short-form video + static variations

Existing 9:16 shorts and static variations, running on LinkedIn + Meta.

Each message is paired with the corresponding case study on the website as destination.

02 · Retarget

38-sec testimonial cut

LinkedIn + Meta engaged audiences (ad and website) + YouTube retargeting off website visitors, reinforcing brand awareness and building consideration.

Creative formats

Video: Long-form 38-sec cut and 9:16 shorts.
LinkedIn needs both horizontal (16:9) and vertical (9:16) cuts since it isn't a video-first platform; Meta can run on the same square/vertical statics.
Statics: Square + vertical adaptations per video hero asset, using a still/frame from the video plus 2–3 headline variations.
Two reasons to add statics: broader placement coverage, and lower CPMs.
