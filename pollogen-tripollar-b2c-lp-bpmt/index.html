      02
      Why TriPollar

      More Than One Technology

      TriPollar brings professional-inspired beauty technology into everyday life. Instead of relying on a single technology, TriPollar devices combine several — each with its own role — so your routine works on the skin, the contour and the glow at once.

      Multi-frequency RF warms the deeper layers of the skin, Dynamic Muscle Activation works with the muscle layer beneath it, and LED light adds another layer of care. Smart sensors on the STOP and Aura devices keep every session comfortable and consistent.

      07 FAQ

      Frequently Asked Questions

      Still curious? Leave your details and we’ll get back to you.

      How does TriPollar work?
      TriPollar devices combine several technologies. Multi-frequency RF warms the deeper layers of the skin to support its natural firming process; DMA works with the muscle layer for a toned contour; LED light adds another layer of care. Each device uses the combination that suits its area — see the technology map.
      Which TriPollar device is right for me?
      It depends on your goal. STOP Vx2 Gold and STOP Vx2 S are built for face and jawline firming, Aura combines RF, DMA and red LED in one device, Prism focuses on the eye area, and Pose Vx is designed for the body. Try the Find Your TriPollar quiz.
      How often should I use it?
      Each device has its own recommended schedule, which you’ll find in its user guide.
      How long does a treatment take?
      It depends on the device. Prism sessions take 3 to 10 minutes depending on the mode, and Aura delivers an immediate lifting effect in about 6 minutes. Your device’s user guide gives the recommended time for each area.
      Is it easy to use at home?
      Yes. TriPollar devices are designed for home use, with simple steps you can do yourself — no professional needed. Watch the how-to videos.
      What can I expect?
      Many people begin to notice firmer, smoother-looking skin within 6 to 8 weeks of consistent use. Results vary by person and consistency of use.
      Is TriPollar comfortable and safe to use?
      TriPollar devices are designed for comfortable home use. The STOP and Aura devices continuously monitor skin temperature and movement and stop RF when no motion is detected. Always follow the user guide and its contraindications.
