How we work

From brief to feed, in nine steps.

Two client touchpoints, marked in teal: the monthly calendar review and the post approvals. Everything else runs on our side.

Plan01 to 03
  1. 01

    Audit and benchmark

    We review the accounts, competitors, audience and current numbers.

  2. 02

    Strategy

    Goals, content pillars, tone of voice, the role of each platform, and KPIs.

  3. 03

    Monthly calendar

    Built in Airtable around launches, promotions and holidays. The client reviews the month ahead.

    Client touchpoint
Make04 to 07
  1. 04

    Briefing

    Every creator, designer and editor gets a written brief: shot list, brand do's and don'ts, caption angle.

  2. 05

    Production

    UGC, AI video, statics, carousels and trend adaptations, cut for each platform.

  3. 06

    Internal review

    Strategy and account leads check every post before the client sees it.

  4. 07

    Client review

    The client approves or comments on each post in Airtable. Nothing goes live without approval.

    Client touchpoint
Run08 to 09
  1. 08

    Publish and engage

    Posts are scheduled, and the community is managed every day.

  2. 09

    Report and optimize

    A monthly recap, then more of what worked. For Rail Europe, this system took content from concept to live in 2 weeks.
