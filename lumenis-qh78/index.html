The engagement

A rare chance to capture the whole room, at once.

The Users' Meeting gathers Lumenis customers and key opinion leaders from across the Aesthetics and Vision portfolios in a single location. Noetic will run a two-and-a-half day on-site production — a high-energy floor day, trend-native social formats, and a controlled studio day — to build a professional, education-led, social-first content library spanning triLIFT, Stellar M22, FoLix, and OptiLIGHT.

Scope of work

Three formats, one production.

/01
Floor · Day 1
🎤

On-the-floor interviews

A dynamic format that captures the live energy of the event from a branded content area in a high-traffic zone.

  • Spontaneous, rapid interviews and Q&A with walk-up attendees
  • Voice-of-customer and vox-pop clips — first impressions and reactions
  • Behind-the-scenes and event-recap capture throughout the day
  • Light-touch talent wrangling to invite attendees in
/02
Floor · Day 1
✨

Trend-native social content

Lighter, quirky formats built to feel organic on TikTok and Reels — the kind of content that drives engagement precisely because it isn't polished-branded.

  • Trend-native formats: rapid question games, "guess the device," this-or-that
  • Candid segments with the Lumenis sales team and reps on the floor
  • Playful challenges and quick-hit bits designed for shareability
  • Formats adapted live to whatever's landing with the crowd that day
/03
Studio · Day 2
🎬

KOL testimonials

A controlled, studio-style production day of structured testimonials with 6–7 KOLs across Aesthetics and Vision, each in a dedicated time slot.

  • ~6–7 scheduled KOL sessions, each in a dedicated slot
  • A sit-down / standing interview, physician-led narrative
  • A rapid-fire Q&A segment per KOL, cut for short-form and social
  • Professional lighting and audio in a reserved room
  • A detailed run-of-show and call sheet per KOL

The half day. The core crew films across two full days (Day 1 floor + trends, Day 2 KOL studio), with an additional half day held to capture anything outstanding — extra KOL slots, pickup shots, or overflow floor content. Photography coverage is included for the two main days.

Compliance note. triLIFT, Stellar M22, FoLix, and OptiLIGHT are FDA-regulated devices. Talking points, KOL prompts, and final cuts are reviewed against approved Lumenis marketing language before delivery. No before/after or unapproved efficacy claims are included without confirmed clearance, and all on-camera participants sign release and consent forms per Lumenis' direction.
