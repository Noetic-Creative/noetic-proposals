02 · The frame

Search finds the people already looking. Social finds everyone else.

Paid search is a demand-capture channel. It is excellent at meeting someone who has typed “refinance my car loan”, and you already run it well. But most people with a car loan and a 540+ score are not searching today. They are scrolling. That is where we see three connected opportunities.

Challenge / 01

Bring CPA down

On social, cost per acquisition is decided by two things: how good the creative is, and how well the platform understands which applications are worth money. Both can be engineered.

Challenge / 02

Open new channels

Paid social, organic social and email each reach a driver at a different moment. Opening them properly means native creative and a real testing plan, not search ads resized for a feed.

Challenge / 03

Scale without the cost creeping up

Search volume has a ceiling. Social does not, as long as the creative supply keeps pace with spend. Scale is a production problem before it is a media problem.

Service / 01

Paid Social + UGC

Creative is the biggest lever you can pull.

In a restricted category, targeting options are narrow, so the ad itself does the targeting. Real people, plain language and a strong first two seconds bring the right applicant in and keep the wrong one scrolling.

  • Campaign structure built for the credit special category, consolidated so the algorithm has enough signal to learn
  • UGC that opens on the problem (the payment, the rate, the lease ending), not on the offer
  • Separate creative tracks for refinance and lease buyout
  • On-site application flows in place of restricted lead forms
  • Monthly goals agreed up front, reported against every month

Service / 02

The data backend

Teach the platform what a good applicant is worth.

This is the piece Tamir walked through on the call. Out of the box, Meta optimizes for whoever fills in a form. What you want is the person who gets funded. The gap between those two is where CPA is won or lost.

  • Conversion tracking audited end to end: GA4, tag manager, server-side events
  • A custom conversion defined around a completed, qualified application
  • A lead score built with your risk and operations teams
  • That score sent back to the ad platforms as a value, so they bid for quality
  • One dashboard that both teams trust
Featured · The Sorbet scoring loop

From a form fill to a signal

How the loop worked, step by step

  1. Applicant answers a short set of questions.

  2. Answers are scored on a model built with the Chief Risk Officer.

  3. Each score is given a dollar value.

  4. The value is reported to the ad platform.

  5. The platform looks for more people like the best applicants.

04Send back

The value is reported to the ad platform.

At Sorbet, the platforms adjusted within two to three weeks

  • 8State-level campaigns consolidated so the signal could pool

The lead score was nothing fancy. Sending it back to the platform as a dollar value was the game changer.

Service / 04

Email + Lifecycle

The cheapest acquisition is the one you already paid for.

Every channel sends people into an application. Not all of them finish. Email and SMS automations recover that spend, and they lower blended CPA without a dollar more in media.

  • Started-but-not-finished application flows
  • Timing-based journeys, such as a lease approaching its end date
  • Segmentation by product, stage and behavior
  • A/B testing on subject lines, send times and offers
  • Referral and review requests after funding

04 · How we test

Feed the beast. Then let the numbers pick the winners.

The platforms reward accounts that keep giving them something new to learn from. Every ad wears out. The accounts that hold a low CPA while spend rises are the ones with the next batch already in production. So we treat creative as a supply chain with a fixed method behind it.

  1. One hypothesis per asset: a hook, an angle, an audience moment.

  2. Creators and AI turn each concept into many variants, fast.

  3. New assets enter a dedicated test against the current control.

  4. Judged on cost per qualified application, not clicks.

  5. Winners move to the main campaigns. The rest are switched off.

  6. What we learned writes the next brief. The pipeline never empties.
