Caesarstone × Noetic04 · How it's made
How it's made

Real Caesarstone imagery is always the hero. AI only builds what doesn't exist yet: the unfinished site, a gesture, a movement.

  • 1
    Real photography & filmsAsset Bank lifestyle and space shots, and the vertical product films.
  • 2
    The site, from the same cameraThe empty shot, edited back into a construction site, so the frame lines up.
  • 3
    Cut-outs and handsPeople and pets masked so they can step out of the frame. Generated hands, composited over the real site.
  • 4
    Motion & musicHandheld motion from real stills. Original tracks, with cuts on the beat.
  • 5
    Brand type, editableReal Publico and Frutiger in every film, and as vector outlines in Figma.
