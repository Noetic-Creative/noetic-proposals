Why this build is different Baseline read 6 Aug 2026

Of the 99 workflows enabled in the account at the August audit, 96 had no suppression list and 94 had no goal or exit criteria. Every asset in this program carries all four of the following from the day it is created:

Suppression list attached, the single global one built first
Goal criteria, the action that means a contact no longer needs the sequence
Exit criteria, so nobody sits in a workflow indefinitely
Wave cap, enforced by list size, not by intention
Real reply-to on every send, with a named owner
Sent from laboriq.co, never from a third party server or domain

The workflow and list counts were verified in August and not re-read on 9 September, so they are carried as unverified for today.

Watch flags that stop a wave Risk thresholds, not targets
MeasureProceedHold and reduceStop
Hard bounce rateUnder 2%2% to 5%Above 5%
Spam complaint rateUnder 0.1%0.1% to 0.3%Above 0.3%
Unsubscribe rateUnder 0.5%0.5% to 1.0%Above 1.0%
Delivery rateAbove 97%95% to 97%Below 95%

If wave 0 crosses a hold band on any measure, the program stops and the remaining waves do not send pending review. Previous low volume attempts damaged sending trust, so the cost of getting this wrong is already known. This program sets no open or click rate targets. Results are reported as qualified actions, stage progression and pipeline.

What building the lists does not do Two open blockers

Nothing sends until the emails exist, the preflight passes, and LaborIQ gives a separate go for the first wave. Two items block that first send today and are not solved by any list:

Who owns repliesOpenNo lead ownership in the account today. A reply nobody answers loses the contact twice.
What counts as sales qualifiedOpenSQL stage reads zero because self prospected deals skip it. Needs a sales agreement, not a marketing decision.
