01 · Client option

"Share Your Story"

How it works

  • 1

    Email (plain text, sent from each client's existing point of contact) invites users to share their experience with Opmed.

  • 2

    They're directed to a new page on the site: two short prompts, "What did you use before Opmed?" / "What's changed since?"

  • 3

    Every submission automatically enters the swag box raffle.

  • 4

    Selected testimonials get repurposed as social content post launch (a "wall of love" style LinkedIn post).

  • 5

    Optional: the new logo reveal video can be embedded directly in this email.

02 · Prospect option 1

Survey + Raffle

How it works

  • 1

    Email invites prospects to a dedicated page on the new website, built as part of the site (same nav/footer), not a standalone microsite.

  • 2

    A short, professionally framed diagnostic question: "What's your biggest OR scheduling challenge?"

  • 3

    Each answer instantly reveals a tailored insight or stat (pre mapped content, no live data dependency, fast to build).

  • 4

    The page links out to related product/solution pages, encouraging further site exploration.

  • 5

    Submitting contact info at the end enters the raffle.

  • 6

    Qualitative themes can be repurposed later for a LinkedIn recap post (no real time percentages promised).

03 · Prospect option 2

Premium Content + Raffle

How it works

  • 1

    Email offers early access to an exclusive resource, e.g. a short "State of OR Scheduling 2026" brief, hosted on the new website.

  • 2

    Access is gated by a short form (name, email, title), a standard B2B content exchange.

  • 3

    Submission enters the raffle.

  • 4

    Positions Opmed as a thought leader while driving qualified traffic and better lead data (title, facility type).

  • 5

    Best fit for a C suite / OR leadership audience, reads as an executive resource, not a game.

04 · Mechanics

How the Raffle Works

  • •

    One shared raffle mechanic across all channels (client option + whichever prospect option is selected), keeps rules and fine print simple.

  • •

    Entry paths: testimonial submission (clients) / survey completion (prospects opt. 1) / content download (prospects opt. 2).

  • •

    Prize: Opmed swag box (contents/value to be confirmed by Opmed).

  • •

    Winner: random draw after the raffle closes.

  • •

    Legal: fine print/eligibility rules required, Opmed/Noetic legal to confirm no purchase necessary language and prize disclosure.

  • •

    Timeline: raffle window aligned to the email send schedule, opens launch day, closes after the final prospect wave.
