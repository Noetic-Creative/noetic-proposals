Media plan proposal

Liga MX market entry

A LinkedIn strategy to introduce WSC Sports to Liga MX, its owner groups and Mexico's sports broadcasters.

WSC Sports x Noetic Creative · October 2026

01
Context & opportunity

The opening is clear

WSC Sports already powers content for top-tier clubs, leagues and federations worldwide, turning raw match footage into publish-ready content in minutes instead of hours. This campaign introduces WSC to the Liga MX ecosystem.

18 of 18 clubs on LinkedIn

🏟️
Reach

All 18 Liga MX clubs are active on LinkedIn. That makes it the one platform where we can reach every club directly.

Lean teams

👥
Need

Commercial and content teams are mostly in the tens to low hundreds of employees, so the case for automation and asset management is strongest where capacity is tightest.

The timing works

📅
Window

The Apertura 2026 ends with the Liguilla in December, and the winter break before the Clausura 2027 is when clubs review tools. Launching in October puts WSC in front of decision makers before those choices are made.

02
Key message

The content engine behind the world's top clubs, now built for Liga MX.

One idea carried through every stage, format and audience of the campaign.

Tracking & landing page

Tracking

📊
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag on the landing page, with conversion events for page view, form submission and demo request. Meta Pixel set up as a nice to have
  • UTMs following the current structure for other campaigns
  • HubSpot: native and website forms connected to the campaign for CRM tracking

Landing page recommendations

📄
  • In Spanish (Mexico), with a hero built around the key message
  • A section for each of the three personas, with matching proof points
  • Client logos: LaLiga, FIBA, Big 12, THW Kiel, FZ Sports
  • Short HubSpot form with hidden UTM fields
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag and Meta Pixel installed; ideally GA4 events for session engagement
