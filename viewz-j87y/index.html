03 — Root causes

Where quality leaked, and what closes each leak.

Cause

The form stopped filtering.

Trimming the form to name and email lowered friction, which lifted volume, but it also removed the quality gate that company and job title fields provide.

Fix Added a work-email verification opt-in, plus company, job title, website, and company-size fields. The company-size field carries a rule: selecting a bracket above 50 employees disqualifies the lead from converting.
Status Done
Cause

Google is bringing mostly personal emails.

Search and Performance Max are optimizing for conversions, not for business reachability, so most leads arrive on Gmail, Yahoo, or similar.

Fix Block personal email domains on the website’s form.
Status Not confirmed yet
Cause

New brief to refine the ICP.

Changes to geography, company size, ICP focus, and industries are different enough from the initial brief to require a full targeting update.

Fix Update targeting on Meta and Google.
Status Meta sent, awaiting approval
Google: pending to send.
