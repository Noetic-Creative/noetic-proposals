01 · The setup

The attention is there. The buying intent isn't yet.

Our audit found the same pattern in every channel. Google Search spent $8.7K over 90 days for 16 conversions, with 30% of that spend landing on the homepage. Spin.AI's organic visits fell 64% in a year, and most of what was lost came from pages no buyer would search for. The LinkedIn ad account has been quiet for more than five years.

None of that is a reach problem. Spin.AI already appears in 18 of 65 tracked AI answers, and non-brand search clicks convert almost as well as brand. The work is to stop paying for readers and build a clear path for the people who are ready to buy.

This proposal covers how we would do that, what it includes each month, who does the work and what it costs.
