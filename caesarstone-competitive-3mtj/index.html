At a glanceMar to Aug 2026

Cambria outspends. Cosentino out-reaches.

Blended view · all paid activity

Adding both channels, the two brands sit close to parity, with Cambria slightly ahead on investment. Cambria leads in both channels: narrowly in social, clearly in search. Cosentino gets far more reach for its money on social and exits the period with momentum in search.

Cambria

Convert the pro, defend the brand
  • Social runs in flights on Facebook, led by professional sample orders, its top campaign by spend.
  • A gated Kitchen and Bath Planner supports the sample offer, with brand video on top.
  • Search is pure brand defense, run as a spring flight that peaked in June.

Cosentino

Stay visible, own the category
  • Social is an always-on brand presence, almost entirely on Pinterest, at roughly half the cost per impression.
  • Creative is space-led and brand-level, with Silestone and Dekton in context.
  • Search targets generic category terms and accelerated sharply in August.
How to read this report

Investment figures are third-party estimates built from observed ad activity: sampled impressions, keyword visibility and average market costs. They typically understate true spend, especially for Performance Max, Demand Gen and geo-targeted campaigns. Read them as directional. They are reliable for relative scale, timing, channel mix and messaging, not as exact budgets.

Local fabricators on the Cambria name

Cambria

Because Cambria only bids on its own name, its overlap is with regional fabricators and stone yards that bid on Cambria searches to capture shoppers. No major surface brand competes with Cambria head to head in search.
