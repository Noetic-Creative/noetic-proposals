Route 01 · Business hours

Most stores are open when you’re busy, and closed the moment you’re finally free.

In New York, weekdays belong to work. Weekends belong to everything else: brunch, the kids’ games, the gym, the show, the dinner that turns into drinks.

By the time your day finally slows down, every store in the city has already closed.

Now think about something as important as choosing a mattress. You research it. You compare it. And then you need the time to actually lie on it.

So it keeps getting pushed to “next weekend.”

What if the hour that feels too late is actually the perfect one? What if the end of your plans could be the start of a better night’s sleep?

You fill your weekend. We’ll stay open for it.

Route 01.2 · SleePare Time

We’ve been taught that time decides what we can and can’t do.

Coffee at 8 a.m. Lunch at noon. Shopping before the stores close. And 3 a.m. on a Saturday? Definitely not a normal time to walk into a mattress store.

But who made that rule?

At SleePare, the usual laws of time don’t apply on weekends. Time is relative here. Saturday at 3 a.m. feels like 3 p.m. Sunday at sunrise feels like a lazy afternoon. Same lights on. Same team ready. Same beds waiting for you to lie down.

The clocks still work. We just don’t let them decide when you shop.

The creative turn

What if we created a place where time stops mattering? Where 3 a.m. feels as ordinary as 3 p.m., because the experience is exactly the same. And where the only thing worth measuring is how a mattress feels under your body.

The concept

At SleePare, any hour is mattress hour.

Welcome to SleePare Time, where any hour is the perfect hour to try a mattress.

Route 02 · The city that never sleeps

New York wears exhaustion like a badge. Up late, up early, always on. Every brand celebrates that. We’re the one brand telling New Yorkers to stop. We’ll stay up, so they don’t have to.

They call New York the city that never sleeps.

And it’s true. The lights stay on. The trains keep running. The city doesn’t know when to call it a night.

But maybe we’ve gotten something wrong about that expression.

Just because the city never sleeps doesn’t mean you shouldn’t.

The concept

The city never sleeps. You should.

We’re open so you can rest better. Now open 24h on weekends.

Route 03 · Sleepy Tom

Meet Tom.
Tom is always sleepy.

Not professionally. Not medically. Just the most exhausted man in New York. Maybe you’re Tom.

He has a remarkable talent for falling asleep absolutely anywhere. If there’s a horizontal surface, Tom has probably considered it.

There’s just one problem. For someone who sleeps everywhere, Tom has never found the right mattress.

Until now.

Route 04 · The only good 3 a.m. decision

Not every 3 a.m. decision is a bad one.

Mechanic: flip the reputation of the late-night hour. Everyone knows 3 a.m. is when bad decisions happen. The text you shouldn’t send. The online purchase you’ll regret. The ex you almost called. SleePare is the exception: the one 3 a.m. move you’ll be glad you made.
