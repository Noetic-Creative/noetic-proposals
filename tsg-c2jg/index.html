THE THREE CAMPAIGNS

What each one does.

/01 · BRANDED

No changes

Current setup and negative keyword hygiene stay as is — this segment is already working.

/02 · COMPETITOR CONQUESTING

Activating a queued plan

Dedicated campaign for competitor brand term interception, paired with a comparison landing page built around TSG's core differentiators: universal sensor integration and accuracy in cluttered, low-altitude environments.

/03 · DEMAND GEN

Reach, using what we have

Redirects High-Intent Search budget toward reach with proven creative already in hand. YouTube for brand awareness at scale — video is where sensor fusion and accuracy land best. Discover for feed-native visibility. Gmail, a strong fit for B2B, reaching decision-makers directly in their inbox. Also builds the volume needed to properly evaluate a future Performance Max test.

TARGETING & AUDIENCE STRATEGY

Stacked for the right ICP.

Branded & Competitor

Branded: unchanged — strict brand terms.

Competitor: competitor names + comparison-intent modifiers, same geo footprint as Demand Gen.

Geo: current market list + standard exclusions.

Demand Gen — 3 stacked layers

LAYER 1

Custom Segments

Competitor domains, defense trade media, industry portals + core search terms.

LAYER 2

Customer Match + optimized targeting

Seeded from the existing ABM account list / engaged leads, expanded to similar decision-makers.

LAYER 3

Business Decision-Makers

Detailed demographics layered on top to keep the pool business-context, not general public.

PHASED ROLLOUT

Two phases to scale smart.

  1. 01. Launch All three campaigns live for 6–8 weeks — enough time for this low-volume category to produce a reliable read.
  2. 02. Tier split Once markets show differentiated signal, split Demand Gen (and Competitor, if relevant) by Tier — concentrate budget where engagement is strongest. This is also the checkpoint to revisit Performance Max.
