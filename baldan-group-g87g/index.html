01

Four decades of devices, now with a US base to build on

Baldan Group is a Milan-based, family-owned company with 45 years in professional aesthetics, operating in 29 countries across non-invasive technologies, dermocosmetics and nutrition. T-Shape2 received FDA clearance and reached roughly 600 US points of sale in just over a year, and RealSelf named it one of the fastest-growing technologies in the US in 2025. The US represents roughly 15 to 20 percent of sales today, most of it through Artemis.

02

Artemis is a strong channel, not a growth partner

Artemis distributes T-Shape2 in the US on a model built entirely on digital lead generation, with no field sales force. They generate and close leads well, and they will continue to carry T-Shape2. They do not share performance data with Baldan, and the two do not cross into each other's accounts. Baldan's own US effort is focused on Infra Baldan and T-Face, the two higher-end devices.

03

The mandate: better leads, then more of them

You joined as CMO about a month ago. The most common question you hear internally is why sales is no longer receiving the quality of leads it used to, and nobody has a clear answer. The devices and the offer are strong. Your read is that the ad creative and copy are not expressing the benefit to the clinic, and that information about the ideal customer is fragmented. The brief puts lead quality first, volume second, and asks for brand awareness work alongside.

04

What runs today

One performance marketing manager in Italy sets budgets and runs campaigns. Meta is the primary channel; Google was switched on one to two weeks ago. There is no agency producing content; creative comes from the team with AI assistance. Leads land in Zoho, and an export manager picks up each call personally and closes. Every Baldan device ships with Bcube, your partner-clinic app, powered by Aura, which suggests marketing actions, generates Meta creative for the clinic, moves patients to booking and reports in real time.

05

The customer is a specific kind of clinic

The target is the high-end aesthetic clinic or med spa with physicians or qualified medical staff, premium positioning, and the means to invest in top-tier technology. Devices are financed through leasing, which requires an owner who is financially solid and a business that is more than a year old. Conversations sometimes stop because one of those requirements was not understood up front. All US communication must stay within FDA-validated claims.

06

Shape of the engagement, and the elephant in the room

You are looking for a 12-month commitment with a review at six months, and a partner who tells you how much to invest to sell more, measured on ROI. We shared that retainers for work of this shape typically land between $10,000 and $25,000 a month before media, and that you would regroup internally before the next step. We also addressed the fact that Noetic works with Lumenis: same buyer, not a direct competitor in our read, and we committed to confirming with them before anything moves beyond this exchange. Tamir will cover this separately by email.

02 · The frame

The leads did not change. The channel did.

Everything you described on the call, fragmented ICP, creative that talks about Baldan instead of the clinic, one channel carrying the whole funnel, points at the same gap. Meta's algorithm has changed what it rewards: fresh, varied, specific creative that speaks to a defined buyer. When creative goes stale or stays generic, the platform widens its targeting to find cheap conversions, and lead quality falls. That is the pattern you are seeing. The fix is not a better media buyer. It is a creative engine that keeps the message sharp and current, a qualification step that protects sales from the rest, and more than one road into the funnel.

Capability 01 · The creative engine

The creative engine.

Technology as the hero. The clinic as the protagonist.

Your creative references are Dyson, Alma and LPG: product as hero, mechanism made visible, scientific yet accessible, premium and clean. We agree that is the right register for the brand. The open question in your brief is whether it also wins at lead generation, or mainly at awareness. Our answer is both, if it is paired with clinic-ROI creative and tested against it, and if every concept is expressed with enough range to reach each person in the buying committee.

  • Eight people in creative and design, plus copy, video and motion. Concept to delivered-final, in every channel format.
  • Quality in the concept, discipline in the expression: one strong idea, expressed for each buyer, format and claim angle, with human creative direction on every piece.
  • AI-native production handles the expression layer at a fraction of traditional cost, so budget goes to ideas and craft, not resizing.
  • Product-as-hero system: macro photography, 3D and exploded views, mechanism-of-action animation for Infra Baldan and T-Face.
  • Clinic-ROI system: room utilization, treatment menu expansion, patient demand, and the Bcube and Aura story as the included growth engine.
  • Every piece stays within Baldan's FDA-validated claims, with a claims checklist built into the review flow.

Capability 02 · The qualification engine

The qualification engine.

Become the clinic's partner before you become its vendor.

Telling a med spa owner "we have the perfect device for you, leave your details" no longer works. Asking "tell us about your practice and we will show you where you stand against the market" does. The principle is to give the clinic something of value before asking for anything, and to capture what sales needs to know along the way. The practice benchmark quiz we walked through on the call is one way to do that, shown here as a working demo. It is an example, not the plan: the assessment decides which qualification offers fit Infra Baldan and T-Face.

  • Several formats can play this role: a benchmark quiz, a treatment-room ROI calculator, a financing pre-check, a demo or open-day registration with the right questions built in.
  • Whatever the format, each step gives something back: a market insight from credible, legally reviewed sources.
  • Captures exactly the attributes that decide your leasing eligibility and ICP fit, before the first call.
  • Target completion: 15 to 25 percent of starters finish and qualify, based on comparable programs.
  • Scored lead pushed to Zoho with a profile card, so the export manager opens the call informed.
  • The same data becomes your shared definition of a qualified lead and your scoring model, validated with sales.

07 · What we would measure

Quality first, then volume, then pipeline.

Your brief asks for proposed KPIs with targets at six and twelve months. We propose the framework now and set the targets at the end of the assessment, once we have seen the accounts, agreed the qualified-lead definition with your sales team and established a baseline. Targets set before that would be guesses, and a budget too low to produce a real picture would not tell either of us anything.
