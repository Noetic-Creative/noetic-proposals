At a glanceMar to Aug 2026

Two brands, two very different playbooks

Cambria

Cambria
Defend the brand, convert the pro
  • Paid social runs in flights on Facebook, led by professional sample orders, its top campaign, plus a gated Kitchen and Bath Planner.
  • Search is almost entirely brand defense, run as a spring flight that peaked in June and faded by August.
  • Creative follows the same priority: samples first, then gated planning content, then brand awareness.

Cosentino

Cosentino
Own the category, stay visible
  • Paid social is an always-on brand presence, almost entirely on Pinterest, at a much lower cost per impression.
  • Search goes after generic category terms like quartz countertops, and search activity accelerated sharply in August.
  • Creative is space-led and brand-level, with Silestone and Dekton in context rather than offers.

How to read this report

🧭

Competitor investment is estimated from observed ad activity, since platforms do not publish what advertisers spend. Use every figure as directional: it shows scale, trend and relative position between brands, not an exact budget.

  • Paid social covers ads detected on Facebook, Instagram and Pinterest.
  • Google Search covers detected text ads only. Performance Max, Shopping, YouTube and Demand Gen are not captured, so real search totals are likely higher.

Who they meet in the auction

Advertisers that most often appear on the same search terms as each brand.

Local fabricators on the Cambria name

Cambria

Because Cambria only bids on its own name, its overlap is with regional fabricators and stone yards that bid on Cambria searches to capture shoppers. No major surface brand competes with Cambria head to head in search.
