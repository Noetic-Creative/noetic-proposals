Who we are a good fit for

The profile that tends to work

Not every company is a match. These are the signs of a good one.

Size

Roughly 50 to 1,000 employees. Not a two-person startup, but a company with a real marketing budget.

Marketing setup

Has its own marketing team, or at least a marketing lead, not just a founder doing it alone.

Market

Selling in the US, or actively planning to enter.

Budget stage

Open to investing in growth. An existing ad budget is not required.

Decision-maker

One clear contact: a founder, CMO, VP of Marketing or Head of Growth.

Engagement

Looking for a long-term partner across several channels.

Signs worth flagging

How to spot an opportunity

  1. Someone says they are unhappy with their current agency or freelancer.
  2. A founder or marketing lead asks for a recommendation.
  3. A company is expanding, or just expanded, into the US.
  4. Someone says their reporting is slow, or they don't understand what they are paying for.
  5. A company is hiring for marketing roles, or thinking about building a team in-house.
  6. A friend, former colleague or family member works at a growing company that fits the profile above.

You don't need to close the deal or pitch anything. Passing along a name and a short note is enough.

If it comes up

Don't explain what we are. Share what we did.

People remember stories, not service lists. Start simple, then pick the example closest to what the person is dealing with. Say the type of company, not the name.
