Frequently Asked Questions

Can a device be developed under our brand?

Partner branding and product customization can be explored as part of the development brief. Options depend on the selected platform, project scope, and regulatory requirements.

Which technologies can we explore?

Home-use capabilities include TriPollar RF, DMA, LED, galvanic current and electroporation. The team will assess which options are appropriate for your intended product.

Can you support an international launch?

Target markets are considered during project definition, with testing, documentation and regulatory requirements assessed for each intended launch region.

Does an existing FDA clearance cover a customized device?

Not automatically. Changes to design, technology, intended use, or claims may require additional assessment, testing or a new regulatory submission.
