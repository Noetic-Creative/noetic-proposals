Paid media point of view

Is ChatGPT a good opportunity for TriPollar and Geneo?

Short answer

We don't recommend investing in ChatGPT ads for either brand right now. The platform is still very new, there's little proof of what works, and beauty and medical devices fall under OpenAI's health restrictions, which block these ads in most countries outside the US. In practice, that rules out Geneo's markets and leaves TriPollar with, at most, a small US test if the team wants to try it.

Where we do see value is in organic visibility: making sure ChatGPT mentions Geneo and TriPollar when people ask about devices. That's part of our GEO work.

/01

What ChatGPT ads are today

OpenAI started showing ads in February 2026. The platform has grown quickly since then, but it's still in an early testing stage.

OpenAI has already changed its ad policies several times this year, so these rules could still change. Sources: OpenAI ad policies, Search Engine Journal, Curve Compliance, Tru Commerce, Spilno.

/02

What it means for TriPollar

Of the two brands, TriPollar is the one that could make sense, but only for a small test in the US. People are already asking ChatGPT things like "what is the best at-home RF device for my skin", and that's where a TriPollar ad could show up.

US

It could work, as long as OpenAI approves TriPollar as a beauty device. The ads couldn't include any medical or clinical claims.

UK · Spain

Most likely not possible, since OpenAI generally doesn't allow health and device ads outside the US.

Keep in mind

With a low CTR and no benchmarks yet, a test would help us learn, but we can't promise sales or any specific results. We'd also need a US landing page, because ChatGPT ads send people to a link and we need that page to track results with the OpenAI pixel.

/03

What it means for Geneo

For Geneo, paid ChatGPT ads don't make sense right now, for a few reasons:

Markets

All of Geneo's active markets (Belgium, Netherlands, Australia, India and Canada) are outside the US, where health and beauty device ads are generally not allowed.

Audience

We can't target clinics or estheticians, and many professionals use paid or business ChatGPT accounts, where ads don't show.

Category

Professional aesthetic devices are restricted, even in the US.

Organic is a different conversation. One of our GEO goals for this project is for Geneo to show up when someone asks ChatGPT for a professional aesthetic device company or a device partner. We work toward that through content and SEO.

/04

Our recommendation

So, our recommendation is to focus on showing up in ChatGPT organically and leave paid ads aside for now. If the team still wants to try paid, we can run a small TriPollar test in the US.

01

Geneo: no paid ChatGPT budget for now. We keep working on GEO so ChatGPT mentions Geneo when people look for professional devices or partners, and we track this every month.

02

TriPollar: not something we'd prioritize, but if the team wants to test it, the first step is checking with OpenAI that TriPollar can advertise in the US. If they approve it, a 4 to 6 week test with about $2,000 (roughly 500 clicks at a $4 CPC) would tell us if there's real interest before we invest more.

03

Review again in 2027: OpenAI keeps updating these policies, so more markets or categories may open up next year.
