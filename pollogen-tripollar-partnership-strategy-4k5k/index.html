Audience

This page speaks to one audience only: companies looking for a technology partnership, larger businesses that want to bring their own home-use beauty device to market by building on TriPollar's proven RF technology. It is a partnership offer, not a consumer page and not a reseller page. Every section is written for that partnership audience. The page wording itself lives in the companion copy draft; this deliverable covers the strategy, SEO and GEO, keywords and CTA behind it.

01 — Strategy & vision

A partner to enter the category with.

The home-use beauty device category is growing fast, and TriPollar has the proven technology, manufacturing know-how and regulatory track record a new entrant would otherwise spend years building. This page turns that into a partnership offer.

Who the page is for

Larger companies evaluating whether to enter, or expand in, the home-use aesthetic device category and looking for a technology partner to build on. Brands planning a device launch, companies exploring private-label or co-developed products, and businesses that want proven RF technology instead of starting from a blank page.

Note

This is a considered, high-value decision, so the page builds trust and starts a conversation rather than hard-selling.

The core idea
📈

Lead with the market

Open with why the category is worth entering now, then position TriPollar as the partner to enter it with.

🧬

A platform, not a device

TriPollar is a portfolio of technologies, which signals real R&D depth and makes the partnership feel broad and future-proof.

🛡️

Reduce perceived risk

Clearances, clinical validation and manufacturing are already in place. The message throughout: less risk, more speed to market.

🤝

Invite a conversation

The whole page funnels toward starting a private partnership discussion, which is how companies actually evaluate this kind of deal.

Tone

Warm, confident, easy to read.

01
Accessible English

Written for a global, often non-native audience. No jargon, no alarming language, no em dashes.

02
Firm and smooth

The TriPollar benefit words, and we use them. The focus stays on the TriPollar brand and its technology.

03
Company credibility, not parent branding

Pollogen appears as the company behind the technology where it adds credibility. Lumenis is not surfaced on this page.

Guardrails before publishing
  • Source-check every claim. Any clinical or regulatory statement needs verification before it goes live.
  • Market-size figures (category value and growth rate) confirmed against a citable source.
  • The GLP-1 demand trend attributed to a named industry survey.
  • FDA and regional clearances checked per market, stated only where current.
  • The "decades / 50+ years" heritage line attributed to Pollogen / TriPollar history, not Lumenis lineage.
02 — SEO & GEO approach

Be the answer, in search and in AI.

The goal is simple: when a company searches for a home-use device technology partner, or asks an AI assistant the same thing, TriPollar should show up. We get there two ways at once.

One hub, one ring of content

The landing page itself stays focused and fairly short so it converts well. It does not try to rank on its own strength alone. Instead, a small set of supporting articles sits around it, each answering a real question a partner asks, and each linking back to the page.

Why this matters for you: it keeps the page clean and persuasive for the reader, while giving Google the depth it needs to rank us. The heavier technical and evidence material lives in the supporting articles, where it also doubles as sales content.

04 — Calls to action

Give a partner a real next step.

Right now the Pollogen site has no call to action a prospective partner can act on. The only business-facing link goes to a partner login area, meant for partners who already exist, not new ones trying to reach us. In practice, any enquiry the site produces today happens in spite of the site, not because of it. This page fixes that with three clear steps, from lowest to highest commitment.
Primary

Request the partnership overview

A substantive document, not a form that leads to a login wall. It covers the technology platform, partnership models, and regulatory and clinical grounding. A concrete, valuable artefact, and asking for it naturally qualifies the lead. Every section of the page points here.

Secondary

Schedule a partnership conversation

A private discussion with the team. This matches exactly how a company weighing an OEM or co-development deal wants to move: talk first, understand fit, then explore terms. Lower commitment, and it catches serious partners early.

Tertiary

Download the technology & clinical overview

For the evidence-first partner who wants to see the proof before talking. Gated by email only. This is also the piece most likely to get cited in AI answers, because it sits next to the honest clinical evidence.

What we deliberately avoid

"Contact us" (no value, doesn't qualify anyone), "Learn more" (names no next step), and "Find a Distributor" (points the wrong way, that belongs on consumer pages). Every button on this page should offer something specific in return.

This is the rule the wording follows in the companion copy draft, where the only buttons on the page are the three partnership calls to action above.
