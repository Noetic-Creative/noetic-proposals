Intro · Our goals

Sell the journey, not the ticket.

Rail Europe sells European train travel to overseas bookers from its base in Paris. Since October 2025, Noetic has run its organic social, creator program, video editing and paid social across Instagram, TikTok and Facebook.

Four goals, one calendar.

The product only really exists once you are on board. Every goal below comes back to showing that window seat, then turning attention into profitable bookings.

Agreed with the Rail Europe team · Oct 2025 – Jun 2026
01

Profitable paid growth

Scale Meta revenue at a strong return, with campaigns structured around Rail Europe's own profitability model.

02

A calendar that shows the journey

Fill Instagram and TikTok consistently with real routes and real carriages, not stock footage.

03

UGC as a monthly engine

Route-matched creators every month, with reuse permissions and carrier approval written into the brief.

04

Grow the audience on priority routes

Lift reach, followers and engagement on both platforms, led by Northern Italy, London and "Travel like a local".

Meta Ads highlights · Nov–Dec 2025

Paid social that pays its way.

Three signals from Meta over November and December: efficiency, order value and reach. Each one comes with the plan to scale it.

01 / 03 · Meta Ads

Ad spend efficiency

Nov – Dec 2025

ROAS+26%

Meta campaigns delivered exceptional efficiency: every €1 invested generated nearly €46 in revenue, a highly profitable return on a flat budget.

ReadSpend held almost level (+0.28%) while ROAS climbed 26%. The extra return came from the same money working harder.

How we're going to scale it

  • Learn more about Rail Europe's profitability model and restructure the campaigns to deliver against those goals directly.
02 / 03 · Meta Ads

AOV + purchases increase

Nov – Dec 2025
Average purchase value+18%
€231.21
Purchases+9.83%
2,358

An 18% increase in AOV shows we're attracting higher-value customers and moving them toward higher-priced purchases: strong audience quality and effective optimization.

TestWe started testing Advantage+ audiences on Promo campaigns. They bring more purchases at a lower cost than retargeting.

How we're going to scale it

  • Add an Evergreen campaign with an Advantage+ audience to secure a healthy mix.
  • Remarketing captures high-intent users at a great return.
  • Advantage+ feeds the funnel with new, cooler prospects who convert at high volume.
