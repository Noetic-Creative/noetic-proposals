The gap between signing and vesting

A Grant Changes Shape After You Sign It

Nobody reads a grant closely on the day it’s issued. By the time someone does — at a vest, an exercise, an audit — it may already be a different instrument under different rules.

Ever-Changing Compliance

Each country has specific regulations around equity, and they constantly change. That exposes you and your employees to financial penalties.

Complicated Tax Codes

Tax codes for equity are extremely complicated. Mistakes are irreversible, and it is easy to miss large tax breaks.

Costly Manual Work

Setting up and maintaining global equity plans is heavily time-consuming, and requires deep and expensive research.
