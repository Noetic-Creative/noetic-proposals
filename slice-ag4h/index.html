01 The setup

The category sells presence. Slice sells certainty.

Carta, Pulley and Ledgy compete on price, migration and breadth. None of them talks about what happens once one grant sits inside two countries' tax rules. That is the gap Slice owns: it keeps working out what each grant owes, including tax treatment, withholding and reporting, as rules and locations change.

The paid accounts don't say that yet. LinkedIn has spent $12,073 for 2 reported leads since July. Google Ads has spent $7,185 for roughly 8 real ones, because a single demo request is tracked five different ways. The spend is reaching roughly the right people. The account structure, tracking and landing experience are what's failing.

Put a Deel or Melio logo on the ad. If it still works, it doesn't run.

That Swap Test is the filter for everything below. The media plan gives the buyer a reason to pay attention. The creative gives them lines only Slice can say. The landing pages give them a next step that fits where they are in the funnel.

02 What the audits told us

Five findings. Each one fixable.

01

Google Ads · MeasurementThe account can't see its own leads.

One demo request fires five conversions. Brand reports 16 when the real number is 5 or 6. CRM stages never reach Google, and the form accepts personal emails.

Fix: one primary conversion, a working HubSpot import, and a single-step form that only accepts business emails.

02

LinkedIn · FunnelCold audiences get asked for a demo on the first touch.

July went straight to lead gen with no ramp-up and no retargeting. August lifted CTR from 0.52% to 1.49% and engagement from 0.7% to 2.96%, but lead form conversion stayed flat at 0.8%.

Fix: document ads and video at the top of the funnel, retargeting built from engagers and key-page visitors, and a demo ask only for warm audiences.

03

LinkedIn · AudienceBudget leaks to people who will never buy.

Job function and title data broadly match the ICP. Small shares still go to BD, Sales, Education, entry-level titles and companies under 50 people.

Fix: two lanes. Core ICP covers Finance, HR and Legal at Series A+ companies with teams outside the US. Partner channel covers VC, startup law, boutique CPA and consulting firms.

04

Google Ads · DemandNon-brand bought the wrong searches.

Non-brand spent $5,279 in 17 days, mostly on employee login portals, mechanical pulleys and trading terms. "Global equity compliance" is almost never searched. The real demand is in category terms like equity management software (~820/mo) and cap table software (~1,140/mo).

Fix: 8 shared negative lists, three exact-match ad groups, and a dedicated Carta group (~810 comparison searches a month).

05

Destination · CreativeGood traffic lands on a page built for everyone.

Paid traffic lands on the main site, which has competing CTAs and two different lead forms. The testimonial video is the strongest asset in the account and it's under-produced. The brand video runs in the wrong aspect ratio.

Fix: purpose-built landing pages for each lane and campaign, designed and written by Noetic, plus a steady supply of UGC built for the feed.
