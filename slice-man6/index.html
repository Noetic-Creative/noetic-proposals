The new frame

Three things that are true of equity and false of payroll.

It comes due later

Salary is finished every month. A grant made today gets tested at a vest, an exercise or an exit, years from now, under whatever rules apply by then.

Feeds directions 1 and 4

It changes identity by country

The same 10,000 options are an ISO in the US, EMI in the UK, BSPCE in France, VSOP in Germany, Section 102 in Israel. A salary is a salary everywhere.

Feeds direction 2

The record isn't the obligation

A cap table shows who owns what. It doesn't show the withholding, filings and deadlines each grant creates. Payroll has no such gap.

Feeds direction 3

Direction 4

Every grant gets read eventually.

Insight

Nobody reads a grant closely on the day it's issued. Years later someone will: an acquirer's counsel, an auditor, a tax authority, a departing employee's lawyer.

How the story runs

Each execution dates a grant twice. Slice reads every grant first, against local law, the moment it's made. And it has already read the old ones.
