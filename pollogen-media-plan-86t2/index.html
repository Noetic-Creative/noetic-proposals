Why this shape

  • English-speaking markets first. Geneo distribution is available across the UK, Germany, Australia, New Zealand, and the US. Other markets value content in their local language, so we suggest focusing the budget on the English-speaking countries where one set of content works well for everyone.
  • Meta leans B2B outside the US. The big US competitors focus their clinic advertising in the US, so B2B is more open and winnable in the other markets.
  • The US stays 100% B2C. Competitors speak mostly to clinics there, leaving the consumer lane wide open at a lower cost. Consumer demand then pulls through to clinics.
  • Two TikTok campaigns because the account is new: one to grow followers, one for lower-cost engagement.

Why this shape

  • English-speaking markets, UK and US. One set of content works well across both. Other markets value content in their local language, so we suggest focusing the budget on the English-speaking countries, with the US as the growth priority thanks to an active main distributor there.
  • Premium targeting. As a higher-priced device, we filter for high spending power and, in the US, top-income areas. UK premium targeting relies on prestige-brand interests and price self-selection.
  • Audience age 25 to 49 — established enough to invest in a premium device, without reading too old for the brand.
  • Two TikTok campaigns because the account is new: one to grow followers, one for lower-cost engagement. TikTok's B2C focus aligns closely with TriPollar, so it carries the larger share of the budget.

Good to know

How the numbers were set

  • Budgets are built on real results from a comparable Lumenis-family brand with a similar target, so the spend levels and channel splits are proven, not guesses.
  • Meta carries the majority for best cost-per-engagement and reach; TikTok takes a healthy but smaller share.
  • Geneo consumer budget starts even across English markets and the US. Once we see which converts better, we shift budget toward it.
  • Everything is a starting point. We watch performance in the first weeks and scale what works before increasing spend.
