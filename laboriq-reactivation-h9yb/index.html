01

Where the list came from

Bulk import and append work, concentrated in 2020 and 2021, with almost no organic engagement.

Created in 2020 and 2021
69.1%

132,504 of 191,724 contacts were created in one 24 month window. The import log shows a single day, 21 July 2020, that added 107,179 of them.

A list that grows this way was loaded, not earned.

Trace to offline sources
97.9%

187,611 contacts entered through imports, manual entry or integrations. Only 4,113 came through a tracked channel such as search, paid or referral.

HubSpot's Original Source property, used as the closest available proxy for imported versus built.

Never interacted
95.2%

182,598 contacts have never submitted a form or viewed a page. 135,408 of them were never sent a tracked marketing email at all.

Silence here means never mailed, not uninterested.
