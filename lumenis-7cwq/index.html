The engagement

A tight, creator-led content batch for triLIFT 2.0.

This proposal outlines a one-off engagement to produce user-generated content for triLIFT 2.0. Noetic develops the creative briefs, manages the relationship between your clinics and the creators, coordinates the shoots, and delivers lightly edited, platform-ready video. Lumenis provides the participating clinics.

Why this works

Why UGC is right for triLIFT 2.0.

🤝

Trust, from a peer

Aesthetic buyers — MedSpa owners and core physicians — trust real practitioners far more than polished brand ads. Creator content lets triLIFT 2.0 be seen through the eyes of people the audience already relates to.

📈

Built for engagement

UGC consistently outperforms produced brand content on interaction — the primary KPI here. Native-feeling, creator-led video is what earns saves, shares, and comments across TikTok, Reels, and organic social.

♻️

A versatile library

One batch produces assets you can boost as paid, drop into organic calendars, hand to sales, or repurpose across the funnel — flexible, on-brand content with a long shelf life well beyond the initial post.

Compliance note. triLIFT 2.0 is an FDA-regulated device. All creator scripts and final cuts are reviewed against approved Lumenis marketing language before delivery. No before/after or unapproved efficacy claims are included without confirmed clearance.

Project timeline

What to expect, start to finish.

An indicative flow once the engagement kicks off. Exact dates are set together based on clinic and creator availability — a typical batch runs about six weeks end to end, since creator sourcing and scheduling naturally take time.

Week 1
Kickoff & briefs

We align on video count, angles, and the clinics you're providing, then build the creative direction and per-creator briefs for your review.

Week 1–3
Creator sourcing & approval

We source and vet creators, match them to clinics, and bring our shortlist to you for sign-off before anything is booked. This is where most of the lead time lives — good creators are worth the wait.

Week 3–5
Coordination & shoots

We handle scheduling and shoot logistics between creators and clinics, then collect the raw footage as it comes in.

Week 5–6
Editing & review

We edit, caption, and QA each video against brand and compliance standards, then share for your feedback and one round of revisions.

Week 6
Final delivery

The finished, ready-to-post batch is delivered in social-ready formats in one consolidated handoff.
