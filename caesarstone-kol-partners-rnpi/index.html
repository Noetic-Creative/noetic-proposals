Partners UGC

What is Partners UGC?

Designers, architects and fabricators who have already created content with Caesarstone and tagged us. Professionals who are active on social media.

How it works
  1. 01

    We select

    We review the professionals who have tagged Caesarstone and shortlist the best fits.

  2. 02

    We propose

    We send them a message proposing a content collaboration, at no cost.

  3. 03

    We brief

    They receive a content idea, for example a video answering a specific question, with a brief covering the question, video guidelines and delivery details.

  4. 04

    They create, we share

    They film the content, and we publish it on Caesarstone's page with them added as collaborators.

The partner getsVisibility for their work and expertise across Caesarstone's community.
Caesarstone getsSocial proof from real professionals who choose and specify the product.

Content ideas

Content ideas

/01

Expert Q&A

We send the partner 2–3 simple questions about design, materials or their expertise. They record short talking-head answers, and we edit the raw footage into a dynamic Reel.

"What's one thing homeowners should consider when choosing a kitchen surface?"
/02

Project Walkthrough · Why I Chose Caesarstone

The partner films a quick walkthrough of a completed Caesarstone project and explains which surface they chose, why, and how it works in the design. Very accessible, since they can film a project they already have.

/03

Quick-Fire: Professional Edition

A short, social-first Q&A with fast answers about the partner's preferences and expertise. Strong Reels potential, since editing makes simple raw footage feel dynamic.

"Bold or minimal? Warm or cool tones? Favorite Caesarstone surface? Current favorite kitchen trend?"
/04

One Question, Multiple Experts

We send the same question to several partners. Each records a 5–10 second answer, and we combine them into one Reel. A tiny commitment per partner, while the final piece feels bigger and reinforces Caesarstone's professional community.

"What kitchen trend are you seeing everywhere right now?"
/05

Designer / Fabricator Tips

The partner shares one practical tip from their area of expertise in a short talking-head video. Useful, saveable content that positions the partner as the expert, so it never feels like an ad.

"One mistake to avoid when choosing a countertop."
