Key points

What we’re seeing so far

  • Engaged contacts are opening at 34.9%, well above HubSpot’s 20% benchmark, and the second email held up as well as the first.
  • Zero spam complaints across 7,302 emails, and the HubSpot health score has held at 3 while volume grew about 13 times.

Key points

What the numbers say

  • Engagement level drives results. Engaged segments open about 7 times more than low-engaged, which is why the plan sends them first.
  • ICP opens more. A1 opens at 41.9% against 31.8% for B1, with the same emails.

  • Openers in A2 are motivated. Click-through reached 13.6% in batch 2, the highest of any send.
  • Delivery improves as the list is cleaned. Every bounce is removed right away, so later sends to the same people bounce less.

The care behind the numbers

How the list is protected

  • Cleaned before sending. 42,350 contacts removed up front, and silent contacts with no history left out.
  • Gradual ramp. The 461 most engaged contacts went first, then bigger waves in batches of 2,000 to 3,000.
  • Suppression after every send. 1,671 contacts excluded so far for bouncing, unsubscribing, reporting spam or opting out, including addresses HubSpot flagged before this campaign.
  • Checks between batches. After A2’s first batch, sends paused to clean the rest of the list. Batch 2 bounced less: 4.5% vs 5.9%.
  • Exits built in. Contacts leave the journey if they convert or start a free trial.
