Approach Per Brand

Two accounts,
two playbooks.

Geneo Global

Professional aesthetics, consumer-facing on TikTok

TikTok audience is 100% B2C. Consumer interest pulls demand through to clinics. Credible, results-driven, warm.

What we post

Treatment results and mechanism explainers, esthetician and clinic moments, glow and oxygenation storytelling, myth-busting on professional skincare. Recurring series make the feed recognizable.

Who we target

Skincare and beauty-tech creators, estheticians and derm-adjacent educators, beauty media. Micro and mid-tier first for authenticity and cost.

Trends

Only formats and sounds that fit a credible professional brand. We participate where it is authentic, not on every trend.

Voice guardrails

Always "Geneo [Name]", never a standalone treatment name. "Glow" belongs to Geneo. Any clinical claim reviewed for regional regulations before publishing.

TriPollar

Premium home-use device, fully consumer

100% B2C across channels. Restarting after a dormant period. Warm, playful, premium. Carries the larger TikTok share in the media plan.

What we post

Home-use ritual content, firm and smooth benefit storytelling, device-in-hand demos, before and during routine moments, relatable lifestyle formats. Recurring series build recognition.

Who we target

At-home beauty and lifestyle creators, premium self-care voices, device and gadget reviewers. Micro and mid-tier for genuine UGC and seeding.

Trends

Playful consumer trends that suit a premium at-home brand. Fast to test, quick to drop what does not land.

Voice guardrails

Keep "home use" explicit. Core words "firm" and "smooth". Avoid "skincare", "real", "best" and clinical claims that could trigger Meta flagging. Anti-aging earned, safer for organic than paid. Never "glow".

The 30-Day Plan

Week by week.

The same rhythm runs across both accounts. Each week ends with a review of what we learned and what we optimize next.

01Foundation

Launch, establish presence, start listening

Posting
  • Feed population: one post per day per brand to give each account an active, credible first impression
  • Lead with the strongest recurring-series concepts
Growth & community
  • Begin strategic following of priority accounts per brand
  • Start the creator discovery list (micro and mid-tier)
  • Monitor trends and sounds; log what fits
02Settle

Move into regular cadence, test formats

Posting
  • Settle into 2 to 3 posts per week per channel within the 18-post cap
  • Test two to three formats per brand against early data
Growth & community
  • Continue strategic following and light engagement (30 to 45 min/week)
  • Grow creator list; flag first outreach candidates
  • First response content from early comments and questions
03Sharpen

Double down on what is working

Posting
  • Lean into the formats and series showing early traction
  • First community-driven videos from real questions
Growth & community
  • Shortlist creators for collaborations, UGC and seeding
  • Identify early organic performers as boosting candidates
  • Cross-channel: repurpose top TikTok content to Instagram
04Optimize

Review, optimize, prepare to scale

Posting
  • Refine the recurring series into a repeatable month-two slate
  • Profile optimization: bio, pinned videos, first-impression view
Growth & community
  • 30-day review per brand against KPIs
  • Hand strong organic performers to the boosting plan
  • Confirm creator outreach priorities for month two

🔬 What we test

  • Hooks and first three seconds
  • Format types: demo, talking-head, trend-led, educational
  • Posting times and frequency within the cap
  • Series concepts against standalone videos
  • Sound and caption styles per brand

🧠 What we expect to learn

  • Which formats earn reach and saves per brand
  • What each audience actually engages with
  • Which creators and topics are worth pursuing
  • Which organic content is strong enough to boost
  • How to shape the month-two calendar
